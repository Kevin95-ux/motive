Get answers for your specific home

A window consultation may help you review available product configurations, measurements, installation needs, project timing, and next steps.

  • Available window styles and configurations
  • Glass, frame, color, grid, and hardware options
  • Installation and project preparation
  • Written warranty and financing information

Homeowners commonly explore replacement because of drafts, difficult operation, deteriorating frames, moisture between panes, outside noise, outdated appearance, or a desire for better light and ventilation. A participating provider can inspect the openings and discuss an appropriate project path.

Depending on provider and market, available styles may include double-hung, single-hung, casement, awning, sliding, picture, bay, bow, garden, shaped, hopper, impact-rated, and other specialty windows.

Available products may include insulated frames, multiple panes, low-emissivity coatings, gas-filled insulating spaces, warm-edge spacers, weatherstripping, reinforced rails, and climate-specific glass packages.

Many replacement-window projects use products sized for the existing openings. Depending on the provider, homeowners may review colors, finishes, grid patterns, glass, hardware, screens, specialty shapes, and mixed fixed or operable configurations.

A provider may review your existing windows, discuss project goals, examine openings, confirm measurements, explain available products, review installation considerations, and prepare project-specific pricing or recommended next steps.

The process may include protecting the work area, removing the old unit, inspecting and preparing the opening, fitting the new window, insulating and sealing around the frame, completing trim, cleanup, testing, and a final walkthrough.

Timing depends on window count, style, custom sizing, product availability, permits, opening conditions, weather, installation complexity, and the provider’s current schedule.

Follow the manufacturer’s written care instructions. General maintenance may include approved glass cleaning, keeping tracks and drainage paths clear, correctly operating locks and sashes, and periodically inspecting screens, weatherstripping, caulk, and hardware.

Warranty availability and coverage vary by provider, manufacturer, product, and installation agreement. Request the full written warranty and review components, labor, exclusions, registration, transferability, service fees, and claim procedures.

Some participating providers may make financing available through third-party lenders. Financing is not guaranteed and may depend on application, approval, creditworthiness, lender terms, project eligibility, and geographic availability.
