This Privacy Policy explains how Motiv Brands Group, LLC, Motiv Brands, Inc., Leads Motiv, LLC, and their controlled subsidiaries and affiliated consumer-facing brands (collectively, "Motiv," "we," "us," or "our") collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information.

Window Motiv is a consumer-facing marketing brand and DBA of Motiv Brands Group, LLC.

Motiv operates a network of consumer-facing websites, landing pages, forms, marketing brands, and related services. Leads Motiv, LLC is the primary entity through which many of Motiv's consumer-facing marketing brands and DBAs are used for lead generation. A current list of our subsidiaries, brands, and DBAs is available through our Brands, Affiliates, and Marketing Partners Disclosure.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our websites, landing pages, forms, telephone communications, text messages, emails, advertising interactions, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Not every Motiv brand collects every category of information described in this Policy. The information collected depends on the Service you request and the information you choose to provide.

1. OUR BUSINESS MODEL

Motiv operates consumer-facing brands that help individuals request information, services, quotes, qualification reviews, consultations, or connections with service providers.

Depending on the Service, we may: provide a service directly; help determine whether a consumer may qualify for or be interested in a particular service; match a consumer with an appropriate service provider; transmit a consumer's request to a selected service provider; facilitate communications between consumers and providers; or receive compensation in connection with a consumer-requested referral or lead.

When we provide a lead to a third-party service provider, the completed lead is intended to be provided exclusively to one selected provider for the specific service requested by the consumer, unless we clearly disclose otherwise and obtain any required authorization.

We may use automated routing, matching, ping/post, availability, or bidding technology to identify an appropriate provider. Limited information may therefore be processed through routing systems or prospective providers to determine eligibility, availability, geographic coverage, pricing, or fit before the completed lead is delivered to the selected provider.

Our agreements generally prohibit a selected provider from reselling or redistributing a Motiv lead to another lead buyer.

A current list of companies that may receive consumer leads is available through our Marketing Partners Disclosure.
