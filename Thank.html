What happens next

Your request may move through several network stages

The exact path depends on your location, project type, window count, provider availability, campaign requirements, and the information supplied with the request.

01

Submission review

The request may be checked for completeness, location, replacement intent, window count, duplication, and technical validity.

02

Coverage evaluation

The project location and details may be compared with available provider coverage, campaign rules, and current capacity.

03

Network matching

An eligible routing, communications, appointment, or service participant may be selected to continue the inquiry.

04

Project conversation

A participant may contact you to verify details, discuss products, request measurements, or explore a consultation.

Prepare for the conversation

A few details can make your project review more productive

You do not need to have every answer ready, but gathering basic property and project information can help a provider understand your goals.

✓
Confirm the approximate window count

Note how many windows you may want replaced and whether the project could include additional openings.

✓
Identify your main concerns

Examples include drafts, difficult operation, condensation, damaged frames, appearance, noise, or efficiency.

✓
Think about project timing

Consider whether you are researching, planning within a few months, or trying to address a more immediate need.

✓
Prepare questions for the provider

Ask about products, installation, written pricing, warranties, financing terms, timing, and who performs the work.

Consumer guidance

Review the provider and written project terms before proceeding

You remain free to compare estimates, ask questions, decline an offer, or choose another provider.

Verify the company

Confirm the provider’s legal name, contact information, licensing or registration where applicable, insurance, and service location.

Get details in writing

Review measurements, products, installation scope, pricing, exclusions, cancellation terms, warranties, and financing disclosures.

Compare your options

A project request does not require you to purchase. You may request another estimate, compare products, or decide not to proceed.
